Sunrise Alarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-18

What we collect and why

  • Location (approximate/precise): Calculate local sunrise time and schedule alarms.
  • Notifications / Exact alarms: Deliver alarms at the correct time even when the app is closed.
  • Audio controls: Temporarily raise media volume for the alarm and restore afterward.

We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone, etc.).

Third-party services

We request sunrise time from the public API at sunrise-sunset.org using your latitude/longitude over HTTPS. No account identifiers are sent.

Data retention and sharing

  • No server-side storage of your location by us; it is used ephemerally to compute sunrise.
  • We do not sell data. Sharing is limited to the API call above.
  • The app may store settings (e.g., language/theme/sound) locally on your device.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Only minimal data (latitude/longitude) is transmitted without user identifiers.

Your choices

  • You may disable location in device settings (functionality will be limited).
  • You may disable notifications/exact alarms in system settings (alarms may not fire precisely).

Contact

Email: support@sunrise-alarm.app

Changes

We may update this policy and will adjust the date above when changes occur.
